## 1. NAVBAR - COMPLETE REDESIGN

### PuzzleFree.game Navbar:
- **Logo**: Minimal text logo "PuzzleFree" (left side, simple)
- **Layout**: Clean horizontal layout with good spacing
- **Navigation**: Simple text links (Browse Puzzles, Daily Puzzles, etc.)
- **Auth**: Login/Register buttons on RIGHT (simple, clean)
- **Colors**: Dark background (#0f0f1e or similar) with light text
- **Styling**: Minimal, no emoji icons, no colorful elements

### AmAha Current Navbar Issues:
❌ Colorful logo (pink/blue) - too playful, not professional
❌ Multiple feature tabs (Quizzes, Puzzles, Stories, Games) - cluttered
❌ Emoji icons (📝 🧩 etc) - looks childish
❌ Admin button visible - confusing for regular users
❌ Dark/Light toggle button - takes up space
❌ Overall looks "busy" and not minimalist

### SPECIFIC CHANGES TO MAKE:
1. **Simplify navbar to:**
   - Logo text "AmAha" (simple white text, no colors)
   - Browse link
   - Categories dropdown
   - Sign In / Sign Out buttons (right side)
   - NO feature tabs (Quizzes, Puzzles, etc.)
   - NO emoji icons
   - NO Admin button in navbar
   - NO Dark/Light toggle in navbar
