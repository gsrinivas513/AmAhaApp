# Interactive Activities Overview

Interactive Activities are self-contained, short-form learning experiences that combine elements of quizzes and puzzles. They're designed for quick, engaging practice and skill development.

## What are Activities?

Activities are mini-games and interactive challenges that:
- Require **2-5 minutes** to complete
- Develop **specific skills** (memory, speed, pattern recognition)
- Provide **immediate feedback**
- Offer **progressive difficulty**
- Support **skill-based leaderboards**

Unlike quizzes (knowledge-testing) and puzzles (logic-solving), activities focus on **skill development through repetition and speed**.

## Key Characteristics

| Aspect | Activities | Quizzes | Puzzles |
|--------|------------|---------|---------|
| **Duration** | 2-5 min | 10-20 min | 10-30 min |
| **Purpose** | Skill building | Knowledge test | Logic solving |
| **Interaction** | Speed/accuracy | Question response | Manipulation |
| **Scoring** | Points/level | Correct answers | Time/hints |
| **Engagement** | Arcade-style | Academic | Challenge-based |

## Why Activities Matter

### Skill Development
- **Speed** - React quickly (Reflex challenges)
- **Memory** - Retain information (Memory games)
- **Pattern Recognition** - Identify relationships (Matching)
- **Organization** - Sort and arrange (Ordering)

### Engagement
- **Quick wins** - Complete in few minutes
- **Leaderboards** - Track progress and rank
- **Achievements** - Earn badges for milestones
- **Replayability** - Daily challenges, variations

### Learning
- **Spaced repetition** - Practice same skills daily
- **Gamification** - Make learning fun
- **Immediate feedback** - Know performance instantly
- **Progression** - Get harder with improvement

## Activity Categories

AmAha features **4 main activity types**:

### 1. **Ordering Activities**
Arrange items in correct sequence.

**Examples:**
- Arrange numbers in ascending order (speed test)
- Order events chronologically
- Sort words alphabetically
- Arrange steps in a process

**Skills:** Logic, sequencing, alphabetization  
**Time:** 2-3 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert

---

### 2. **Matching Activities**
Connect related items or pairs.

**Examples:**
- Match capitals to countries
- Match words to definitions
- Pair pictures with descriptions
- Match synonyms and antonyms

**Skills:** Memory, vocabulary, association  
**Time:** 3-4 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert

---

### 3. **Free-Play Challenges**
Open-ended activities without fixed solutions.

**Examples:**
- Create a story (word limit)
- Design a pattern
- Compose a sequence
- Build a structure

**Skills:** Creativity, expression, problem-solving  
**Time:** 5-10 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Open-ended

---

### 4. **Memory & Reflex**
Speed-based challenges testing memory and reaction time.

**Examples:**
- Simon Says (repeat color sequences)
- Memory Flip (match pairs quickly)
- Reaction Trainer (tap targets fast)
- Number Sprint (recognize numbers quickly)

**Skills:** Speed, accuracy, memory  
**Time:** 2-5 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Progressive (gets faster)

## Scoring System

### Points
- **Correct answers:** 10 points each
- **Speed bonus:** Extra points for quick completion
- **Combo multiplier:** 2x, 3x for consecutive correct

### Accuracy
- **Percentage correct:** Displayed prominently
- **Time taken:** Secondary metric
- **Attempts:** Track retries needed

### Ranking
- **Personal best** - Your best score ever
- **Daily best** - Best score today
- **Weekly leaderboard** - Top 100 this week
- **All-time leaderboard** - Global rankings

---

## Difficulty Levels

Most activities offer 4 levels:

| Level | Speed | Complexity | Time | Audience |
|-------|-------|-----------|------|----------|
| **Easy** | Slow | Simple | 2 min | Learners |
| **Medium** | Normal | Moderate | 3 min | General |
| **Hard** | Fast | Complex | 4 min | Advanced |
| **Expert** | Very Fast | Very Complex | 5+ min | Masters |

Each level has **separate leaderboards** for fair competition.

## Features

### 1. **Daily Challenges**
New activities every day:
- Different difficulty each day
