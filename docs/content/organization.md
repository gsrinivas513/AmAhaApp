# Content Organization

This page explains how content is organized and structured for optimal user experience and management.

## Organization Principles

Content is organized using **3 complementary systems**:

### 1. Hierarchical Organization
Clear parent-child relationships for browsing

### 2. Metadata/Tagging System
Flexible categorization for search and filtering

### 3. User-Centric Grouping
Collections based on learning paths and goals

    ↓

    ↓

## Content Availability

### Visibility Levels

**Public:**
- Available to all users
- Indexed in search
- Appears in recommendations
- Accessible without login

**User-Specific:**
- Available only to specific user
- Not in general search
- Personal assignments
- Custom content

**Group-Specific:**
- Available to classroom/group
- Indexed for group members
- Teacher assignments
- Restricted access

**Scheduled:**
- Available on specific dates
- Time-gated content
- Event-based availability
- Seasonal content

## Quality & Curation Levels

### Verified Content

**Levels:**
- **Unverified** - New content, not yet reviewed
- **Verified** - Checked for accuracy
- **Featured** - High quality, recommended
- **Expert Curated** - Reviewed by specialist

**Display:**
- Badge shows verification level
- Filters can limit to verified only
- Trust indicators help users choose

```
Browse
├── By Grade (my class)

### For Parents
```
Browse
├── By Grade (child's level)
├── By Age (age-appropriate)
