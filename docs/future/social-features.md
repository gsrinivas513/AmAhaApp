# Social Features

Community and collaboration features planned for future development.

## Peer Collaboration

Study groups and collaborative learning features allowing students to work together on puzzles and challenges.

## Social Leaderboards

Compete with friends on leaderboards with:
- Friend-only rankings
- Challenge invitations
- Score sharing

## Achievement Sharing

Share completed challenges, high scores, and achievements across social platforms.

## Discussion Boards

Community forums for:
- Q&A discussions
- Strategy sharing
- Hint exchanges
- Community contests
