# Monetization Models

Planned monetization strategies for sustainable growth.

## Premium Content

Exclusive premium quizzes, puzzles, and activities available through premium subscriptions.

## Subscription Tiers

Tiered subscription model offering:
- **Free Tier:** Basic content access
- **Premium Tier:** Advanced content and analytics
- **Professional Tier:** For educators with class management tools

## Enterprise Licensing

School and district licenses for educational institutions with:
- Advanced analytics for administrators
- Bulk user management
- Dedicated support
- Custom content integration

## Reward System

In-app achievements and badges with potential rewards and incentives.
