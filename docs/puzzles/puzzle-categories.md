# Puzzle Categories

Puzzles in AmAha are organized into 5 major categories, each with distinct gameplay mechanics and educational benefits.

## 1. Grid-Based Puzzles

Logic puzzles played on a grid where you fill cells following mathematical or logical rules.

### Characteristics
- **Board:** Square or rectangular grid (4×4 to 16×16)
- **Elements:** Numbers, symbols, or colors
- **Rules:** Mathematical constraints
- **Goal:** Fill entire grid correctly
- **Time:** 5-20 minutes typical

### Educational Benefits
✓ Logical reasoning  
✓ Pattern recognition  
✓ Mathematical thinking  
✓ Problem decomposition  
✓ Systematic approach  

### Cognitive Skills
- **Deduction** - Use rules to eliminate options
- **Constraint satisfaction** - Balance multiple rules
- **Optimization** - Find efficient solutions
- **Working memory** - Hold multiple constraints

### When to Use
- Teaching logic and reasoning
- Developing systematic thinking
- Brain training exercises
- Math education (for number-based variants)

### Examples in AmAha
1. **Sudoku** - Fill grid with 1-9, each row/column/box unique
2. **Kakuro** - Cross-sum grid (like crossword with numbers)
3. **Nonogram** - Decode numbers to create pixel art
4. **Killer Sudoku** - Sudoku with additional sum constraints
5. **Futoshiki** - Fill grid with inequality comparisons

### Difficulty Progression

| Level | Grid Size | Rules | Time | Example |
|-------|-----------|-------|------|---------|
| Easy | 4×4 | 2-3 | 5 min | 4×4 Sudoku |
| Medium | 6×6 | 4-5 | 10 min | 6×6 Sudoku |
| Hard | 9×9 | 5-6 | 15 min | Standard Sudoku |
| Expert | 12×12+ | 7+ | 20+ min | Super Sudoku |

---

## 2. Tile-Based Puzzles

Rearrange physical tiles or pieces to match a target state.

### Characteristics
- **Board:** Variable canvas
- **Elements:** Tiles, pieces, or shapes
- **Rules:** Physical/positional constraints
- **Goal:** Arrange pieces to complete image/pattern
- **Time:** 10-30 minutes typical

### Educational Benefits
✓ Spatial reasoning  
✓ Geometric thinking  
✓ Visualization skills  
✓ Fine motor control  
✓ Attention to detail  

### Cognitive Skills
- **Spatial visualization** - Rotate/position in mind
- **Shape recognition** - Identify piece orientations
- **Pattern matching** - Compare to target image
- **Motor planning** - Precise placements
- **Persistence** - Try multiple arrangements

### When to Use
- Developing spatial awareness
- Geometry and transformation lessons
- Building visual memory
- Fine motor skill practice
- Attention and focus training

### Examples in AmAha
1. **Jigsaw Puzzle** - Traditional picture reassembly
2. **Slider Puzzle** - Move tiles to arrange numbers 1-9
3. **Tangram** - Rearrange 7 shapes to form pictures
4. **Block Puzzle** - Fit geometric blocks without overlap
5. **Rotational Puzzle** - Arrange rotating pieces

### Difficulty Progression

| Level | Piece Count | Complexity | Time | Example |
|-------|-------------|-----------|------|---------|
| Easy | 10-15 | Clear shapes | 8 min | 15-piece jigsaw |
| Medium | 20-30 | Mixed colors | 15 min | 30-piece jigsaw |
| Hard | 50-100 | Photo image | 20 min | 100-piece jigsaw |
| Expert | 100+ | Fine details | 30+ min | 500+ piece jigsaw |

---

## 3. Word Puzzles

Language-based puzzles combining vocabulary and logic.

### Characteristics
- **Elements:** Letters, words, or phrases
- **Rules:** Spelling, placement, or matching
- **Goal:** Fill grid or find words
- **Time:** 10-25 minutes typical
- **Language:** English (extensible)

### Educational Benefits
✓ Vocabulary expansion  
✓ Spelling mastery  
✓ Language skills  
✓ Word pattern recognition  
✓ Attention to detail  

### Cognitive Skills
- **Lexicon access** - Recall word meanings
- **Pattern matching** - Recognize letter sequences
- **Constraint satisfaction** - Fill letters to fit clues
- **Spelling accuracy** - Correct letter positioning
- **Language rules** - Apply grammar patterns

### When to Use
- ESL/language learning
- Vocabulary building
- Spelling practice
- Language arts enrichment
- Cognitive maintenance (for all ages)

### Examples in AmAha
1. **Crossword** - Fill grid with clues (horizontal/vertical)
2. **Word Search** - Find hidden words in letter grid
3. **Anagrams** - Rearrange letters to form words
4. **Word Ladder** - Change one letter per step to reach target
5. **Cloze** - Fill blanks in sentences from word bank

### Difficulty Progression

| Level | Vocabulary | Clue Type | Time | Example |
|-------|-----------|-----------|------|---------|
| Easy | Common | Direct definitions | 8 min | 8×8 crossword |
| Medium | Standard | Some indirect | 12 min | 12×12 crossword |
| Hard | Advanced | Cryptic clues | 18 min | 15×15 crossword |
| Expert | Specialized | Clever wordplay | 25+ min | Large cryptic |

## 4. Pattern Puzzles

Logic puzzles based on sequences, deduction, and pattern completion.

### Characteristics
- **Elements:** Numbers, shapes, or images
- **Rules:** Sequential or logical patterns
- **Goal:** Identify pattern and complete it
- **Time:** 5-15 minutes typical

### Educational Benefits
✓ Pattern recognition  
✓ Logical deduction  
✓ Sequential thinking  
✓ Inductive reasoning  
✓ Math skills  

### Cognitive Skills
- **Pattern detection** - Identify rules
- **Extrapolation** - Project pattern forward
- **Deduction** - Use clues logically
- **Flexibility** - Adapt to different pattern types
- **Speed** - Solve quickly (timed variants)

### When to Use
- IQ/aptitude test practice
- Mathematical reasoning
- Sequence learning
- Rapid problem-solving training
- Cognitive assessment

### Examples in AmAha
1. **Number Sequence** - Continue 2, 4, 6, 8... or 1, 1, 2, 3, 5...
2. **Shape Pattern** - Find next shape in sequence
3. **Deduction Puzzle** - Use clues to determine facts
4. **Matrix Reasoning** - Find missing element in 3×3 grid
5. **Dominoes** - Find matching number on opposite side

### Difficulty Progression

| Level | Pattern Type | Complexity | Time | Example |
|-------|------------|-----------|------|---------|
| Easy | Linear sequences | +/- patterns | 5 min | 2, 4, 6, 8... |
| Medium | Compound | Multiple rules | 8 min | Fibonacci-like |
| Hard | Complex | Hidden patterns | 12 min | Digit sum sequences |
| Expert | Cryptic | Obscure rules | 15+ min | Complex relationships |

---

## 5. Interactive Puzzles

Real-time puzzles with physics, timing, or special rule sets.

### Characteristics
- **Dynamic:** Board changes with user action
- **Rules:** Gravity, physics, or special mechanics
- **Goal:** Reach specific state (score, clear, etc.)
- **Time:** 5-10 minutes typical
- **Reflexes:** Some require quick responses

### Educational Benefits
✓ Strategic planning  
✓ Real-time thinking  
✓ Consequence prediction  
✓ System understanding  
✓ Optimization skills  

### Cognitive Skills
- **Planning ahead** - Predict consequences
- **Optimization** - Find best move sequence
- **Real-time decision** - Adapt quickly
- **System thinking** - Understand mechanics
- **Risk assessment** - Balance options

### When to Use
- Building strategic thinking
- Physics understanding
- Decision-making under pressure
- Game-based learning
- Engagement and motivation

### Examples in AmAha
1. **Block Puzzle** - Stack falling blocks, complete rows
2. **Tower of Hanoi** - Move stacks following rules
3. **Pipe Pipeline** - Connect pipes to route liquid
4. **Sliding Brick** - Move blocks to free exit path
5. **Match-3** - Swap adjacent tiles, match 3+ colors

### Difficulty Progression

| Level | Mechanics | Complexity | Time | Example |
|-------|-----------|-----------|------|---------|
| Easy | 1 rule | Simple layout | 5 min | 5×5 block space |
| Medium | 2 rules | Moderate layout | 8 min | 8×8 block space |
| Hard | 3 rules | Complex layout | 10 min | 10×10 block space |
| Expert | 4+ rules | Intricate layout | 15+ min | Custom rules |

## Choosing a Category

**For Memory Development:** Grid & Pattern Puzzles  
**For Spatial Skills:** Tile-Based Puzzles  
**For Language:** Word Puzzles  
**For Quick Wins:** Pattern & Interactive Puzzles  
**For Deep Engagement:** Grid-Based & Tile-Based Puzzles
